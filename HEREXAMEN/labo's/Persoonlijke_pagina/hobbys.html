Things I love

Everyone has things they enjoy doing in their free time. For me, these hobbies are a way to relax, stay active, and spend time doing things I genuinely enjoy.

Hiking

I love exploring nature and discovering new places on foot. Hiking gives me a chance to get away from everyday life, enjoy the outdoors, and challenge myself with new trails and undiscovered places.

Cycling

Cycling is one of my favourite ways to stay active while exploring new place, whilst trying to cath the horizon. I enjoy going on longer rides, discovering new routes, and simply enjoying the journey along the way.

Reading

I enjoy getting lost in a good story, wether i'm reading a novel, manga or manhwa. I mostly read fiction because I love discovering new stories, characters, and worlds. Reading is one of my favorite ways to relax and spend some quiet time.
