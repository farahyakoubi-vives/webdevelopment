Recently Watched

A collection of some of the latest movies and series I've watched, along with my personal thoughts and ratings.

The Odyssey

The Odyssey was one of those movies that was highly anticipated, especially with Christopher Nolan directing it. I ended up going with my brother.
I didn't watch it "the way Nolan intended". I wasn't about to travel all the way to Brussels just to see a movie in IMAX. To add the seats were already sold out anyway. So I went with the standard cinema experience instead. As for the movie itself... It was PEAK. I enjoyed it from beginning to end.
Rating: 5/5

Spider-Man: Brand New Day

I was really forward to the Spider-Man movie, especially after enjoying the previous movies, I was curious to see where the story would go next.
I went to see with both of my siblings. I always enjoy watching a good movie with some good popcorn!
Rating: 5/5
